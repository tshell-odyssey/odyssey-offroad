Our story

We build what we'd trust
with our own family.

Odyssey Off-Road was built on a simple conviction: if Tyler and Kristen wouldn't put their own family in it, they wouldn't sell it. That's not a slogan — it's the standard every weld, bracket, and component decision gets measured against.

"We do what we say we'll do — even when it's harder, slower, or costs more."

Trailers are tools. But the memories made with them are the whole point. Tyler and Kristen keep that outcome front and center in every design decision — because they're not just building a product, they're building something their family will use for years.
