Our story

Built from experience.
Driven by family.

Odyssey Off-Road didn't start in a boardroom. It started on the trail — with a homemade trailer, four dirt bikes, and a question strangers kept asking: "Where did you buy that?"

Where it started

The trailer that started it all.

About six or seven years ago, Tyler needed a trailer that didn't exist. He wanted to haul four dirt bikes, stay off-road capable, and actually live out of it — with a real under-deck storage drawer big enough to matter and a deck wide enough to pitch a tent on for the whole family. No crawling into a cramped ground tent. No compromises.

So he built one. Starting from a standard trailer, he engineered it from the ground up — the same way he's approached every fabrication project in his career. When he started taking it out on the trail, something unexpected kept happening: strangers would walk up and ask where he bought it.

"I didn't think much of it at first. But close friends kept saying — you should be selling these."

So together, Tyler and Kristen made the decision: build it right, or don't build it at all. Odyssey Off-Road was born.

The people behind it

Tyler

Founder & builder

Tyler holds a degree in civil engineering and serves as VP at one of the region's largest steel fabrication companies. Fabrication isn't a skill he picked up — it's how he was raised. He grew up around metal, welds, and the belief that if something's worth building, it's worth building properly.

Outside of work, life revolves around dirt bikes, the outdoors, and family. Every design decision on an Odyssey trailer comes back to the same question he asks on the trail and in the shop: does this serve the journey?

Kristen

Co-founder & the glue

Behind every great build is someone making sure everything — and everyone — stays together. Kristen is that person. She captures the moments that matter, tells the story of this brand through photos and social media, and keeps the family that started all of this strong and grounded.

No trailer, no business, and no adventure means anything without the people you share it with. Kristen makes sure Odyssey never loses sight of that.

"The brand lives in the build. The soul of it lives in the family."

Why Odyssey exists

Trailers are tools. Memories are the outcome.

Most trailer manufacturers design for the showroom. We design for the single track, the campsite, the morning your kids wake up in a tent pitched on the deck and can't believe where they slept.

What drives Tyler and Kristen isn't a sales target. It's the stories they hope to hear — dads taking their kids out for the first time, families cooking dinner at a trailhead, couples who finally found a rig that keeps up with their lifestyle.

Do it once, do it right

We don't cut corners to hit a price point. We make the harder call — slower, costlier, better — every time.

Built for years, not seasons

Trust is earned over time. We design for the long haul — and stand behind every product long after delivery.

Family first, always

From the founders to the customers — this brand was built around family and the experiences that bring people closer.

The journey so far

2018 — 2019

The original build

Tyler builds the first trailer from scratch — four dirt bikes, off-road suspension, a one-of-a-kind oversized under-deck drawer, and a deck large enough for the whole family to camp on. Strangers start asking where they can buy one.

2024 — LLC founded

The decision is made

Tyler and Kristen officially found Odyssey Off-Road LLC. The goal from day one: build a legacy product, not a quick-to-market one. Do it once, do it right.

November 2024

Design & engineering begins

Initial trailer design kicks off. Every system — suspension, power, modular storage, onboard water — spec'd from scratch with purpose.

Early 2025 — in progress

Prototype in build

The first full Summit prototype is under construction. Frame and chassis complete. Suspension, power systems, and storage fitout underway. Launch expected in 1–2 months.
