01 — Trailer Frame

Completed — Nov 2024 to Jan 2025

Complete▶

The foundation of every Odyssey trailer starts here — and we didn't rush it. The frame was fully designed, cut, and welded over a two-month period, with every joint inspected before moving forward. Tyler's background in civil engineering meant no guesswork on load ratings, tow geometry, or weld integrity.

02 — Trailer Suspension

In progress — started Dec 2024

In progress▶

Getting the suspension right is non-negotiable for an off-road trailer that still needs to be towed comfortably on the highway. We're currently finalizing axle specs and suspension geometry — balancing articulation for rough terrain with stability at towing speeds.

03 — Trailer Deck System

In progress — started Jan 2025

In progress▶

The deck needs to be large enough to pitch a 4–6 person tent, strong enough to handle the load, and light enough not to compromise the tow rating. The structure beneath the deck houses the Trailer Drawer Assembly — these two stages are being developed in parallel.

04 — Trailer Drawer Assembly

In progress — started Jan 2025

In progress▶

This is one of the features that makes Odyssey genuinely different — a full-width, heavy-duty slide-out drawer built into the underside of the deck. No other production trailer manufacturer offers anything like it at this scale. The mechanism, slides, and locking system are all being fabricated in-house by Tyler.

In progress▶

06 — Onboard Water System

In progress — started Feb 2025

In progress▶

Running water changes everything on a multi-day trip. The Odyssey water system is being integrated directly into the trailer structure — speccing tank capacity, pump system, and plumbing routing — all with freeze protection and ease of refilling in mind.
