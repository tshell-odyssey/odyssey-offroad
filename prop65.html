⚠ WARNING

This product can expose you to chemicals including lead, phthalates, and/or other chemicals, which are known to the State of California to cause cancer, birth defects, or other reproductive harm. For more information, go to www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.

What is Proposition 65?

California's Proposition 65, officially known as the Safe Drinking Water and Toxic Enforcement Act of 1986, requires businesses to provide warnings to California consumers about significant exposures to chemicals that cause cancer, birth defects, or other reproductive harm. These chemicals can be in the products you purchase or present in places you visit.

Why Does This Apply to Odyssey Off-Road Products?

Odyssey Off-Road trailers and accessories are manufactured using materials common to the recreational vehicle, automotive, and fabrication industries. Some of these materials — including steel components, coatings, welding byproducts, rubber seals, and electrical wiring — may contain or emit chemicals listed under Proposition 65. This warning is provided out of an abundance of caution and in compliance with California law.

Our Commitment

The Odyssey Team is committed to building products that meet or exceed applicable safety standards. We continually evaluate our materials and manufacturing processes. This warning is a legal requirement and does not mean our products are unsafe for normal intended use.

More Information

For more information about Proposition 65, visit the California Office of Environmental Health Hazard Assessment (OEHHA) at www.P65Warnings.ca.gov.
